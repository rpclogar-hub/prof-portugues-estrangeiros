You know the words. So why do you freeze?

Frequently asked questions

How much does a Brazilian Portuguese tutor cost?

Online Brazilian Portuguese tutors charge from about US$15 to US$85 an hour, depending on platform and experience (public price pages checked in October 2026). My rates are in the price section above, and the first 30-minute session is free.

Is Brazilian Portuguese easy to learn for English speakers?

It is one of the easier languages for English speakers. The U.S. Foreign Service Institute puts Portuguese in its easiest group (Category I), at roughly 600 to 750 class hours to reach professional working proficiency. Pronunciation (nasal sounds, the “r”) is the hard part, and that is what we practice from lesson one.

How long until I can have a real conversation?

It depends on where you start and how often you practice. In the first lesson you'll already use basic phrases out loud. In the free session I'll give you a realistic estimate for your goal.

What's the difference between Brazilian and European Portuguese?

Pronunciation, some vocabulary (ônibus in Brazil, autocarro in Portugal), and everyday grammar, like using “você” instead of “tu.” People understand each other, but if you're going to Brazil, learn Brazilian. I teach only Brazilian Portuguese.

Do I need to know any Portuguese before I start?

No. I teach complete beginners through advanced students.

What time zone do you teach in?

Rio de Janeiro is on UTC−3 all year, with no daylight saving time. That puts Rio 1 hour ahead of New York in summer and 2 hours ahead in winter. Tell me your time zone in the booking form and I'll suggest times that work for you.

Do you teach in English or in Portuguese?

Both. I explain in English at the start and move into Portuguese as you improve.

Why choose a private tutor over an app or a class?

You speak the whole hour, get corrected on the spot, and the lessons follow your goal. Apps can't answer back and classes can't give you that much time to talk.
